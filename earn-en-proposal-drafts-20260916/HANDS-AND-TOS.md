# HANDS + ToS — no auto-bid

Status: **DRAFT_ONLY**. This folder is copy text for a human. It is not a bidder.

**HANDS** means: you read the listing, you rewrite the one specific line, you paste in the official form, you click send. A computer must not be the thing that submits.

## Forbidden in this pack (do not add later either)

Do **not** write or run:

- Auto-apply / auto-bid / “Connects leftover spray” / bulk proposal queues
- Browser automation, headless browsers, scrapers, or extensions that submit faster than a person
- Third-party “Upwork bot”, “Fiverr auto-proposal”, or inbox crawlers
- Upwork API / MCP use to **spam proposals or invites** (Upwork says that stays off-limits even with a key)
- Multiple accounts to dodge rate limits
- Unsolicited Fiverr DMs that promote a Gig to people who did not contact you
- Substantially identical Contra applications blasted across opportunities
- Contact info (email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack) in a pre-contract proposal
- Off-platform payment instructions

Fiverr’s **in-product** first-message auto-reply is a platform feature, not a third-party bot. This pack still does not rely on it. If you ever turn that feature on, keep it as a one-line “I will read this and reply in this inbox” — not a pitch, not a custom-offer dump. Native **quick responses** are templates you insert by hand; personalize before send.

Upwork’s on-site writing aids (if shown) do not change the rule: **you** submit. This pack does not tell you to buy Connects, Boosted Proposals, or extra quotes.

## Official lines this pack is obeying

Read the live page before you argue with a client. Help text moves; the links are the source.

### Upwork — bots / automation

Help: [Use bots and other automation properly](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467-Use-bots-and-other-automation-properly)

Upwork describes bots as scripts, programs, extensions, or services that send requests, collect data, or act **faster or more often than a human**. Tools that “say they were created for Upwork” are not thereby allowed. **Even with an API key, spamming proposals or invites, and scraping, stay off-limits.**

### Upwork — contact info and circumvention

User Agreement §7.2 (communicate on-site; no Means of Direct Contact in a profile, proposal, invitation, or pre-hire message), via [Upwork Legal](https://www.upwork.com/legal). Help:

- [How to keep your contact information safe](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051749534-How-to-keep-your-contact-information-safe-on-Upwork)
- [Circumvention](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511133-Circumvention-and-why-it-s-against-the-rules)

Sharing contact information **before a contract starts** (including in a proposal or interview) is against those rules for ordinary clients. Keep scoping in Upwork Messages. Do not move payment off Upwork.

### Fiverr — mass messages and spam

[Community Standards — Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Community-Standards-Integrity-and-Authenticity) (same list in [Our Community Standards](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32242973123985-Our-Community-Standards)):

- Do not use automated processes (software, scripts, bots, or other means) to send **mass messages**
- Do not contact users with offers unrelated to their Gigs, requests, or orders
- Do not send DMs to **promote** your services or Gigs
- Do not scrape the platform or manipulate ranking / fake traffic

### Fiverr — off-platform

[Off-platform policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665-Community-Standards-Off-platform-policy): no off-Fiverr payments, no bypass, no steering to external contracts, no sharing email / phone / third-party messengers **to take business off-platform**. Portfolio URLs to show skill are a different case (Gig policies allow a website/portfolio link to showcase work). Do not put a mailto or WhatsApp in the paste.

Custom offers: [Creating and managing custom offers](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010559198-Creating-and-managing-custom-offers) — send from the **inbox thread**, after the buyer is in that conversation. Briefs: [Personalized offers (Briefs)](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415608857745-Personalized-offers-Briefs-for-freelancers) — respond or decline **in that UI**; declining a brief is allowed.

### Contra — automated outreach and similar messages

[Terms](https://contra.com/policies/terms), Recruiting and Solicitation Restrictions:

- Direct engagement for a **specific** project
- Contra may limit volume/frequency of messages and postings
- Circumventing those limits with **multiple accounts, automated tools, or coordinated activity** is a violation
- “Substantially similar messages” at scale is the pattern to avoid (their recruiting examples; do not copy that pattern for applications either)

Paid project / proposal UI: [Paid projects](https://help.contra.com/en/articles/9322763-paid-projects). Create and send proposals in the official flow. Do not invent a Contra API bidder here.

## Allowed (this pack)

- Draft in git with placeholders
- One human-personalized paste per listing
- Asking scope questions on-platform
- Declining work that needs scraping, engagement fraud, or off-platform pay
- Linking **public** portfolio / GitHub in the body where the desk allows a portfolio URL
- Stopping at KYC / ID upload (not this folder’s job)

## Length (practical, not a secret ToS number)

Upwork’s own cover-letter guidance is **about 200–300 words or fewer** ([Cover letter tips](https://www.upwork.com/resources/cover-letter-tips)). Snippets 01–02 are written short of that so a personalized sentence still fits. The form’s hard cap is much larger; do not fill it.

Fiverr inbox replies should stay short. Custom-offer description must fit the live form (third-party blogs cite 2,500 characters; **trust the counter on screen**).

## Jobs this seller declines (use snippet 08)

- Like / follow / view / “engagement” automation
- Browser automation or scraping where there is no official API
- Publishing to social without the client’s own approve/gate step
- Anything that needs you to pretend you are in another country
