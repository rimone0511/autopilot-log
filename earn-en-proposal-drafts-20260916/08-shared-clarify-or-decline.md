# 08 — Shared: clarify, decline, stay on-platform

| | |
|---|---|
| Desk | Upwork, Fiverr, or Contra |
| Type | Follow-up snippets |
| Mode | DRAFT_ONLY, HANDS paste |
| When | The listing is vague, the work is out of scope, or they asked to move off-platform |
| When not | As a substitute for filling `{{ONE_SPECIFIC_DETAIL}}` on a listing you have not read |

Use **one** block. Do not chain all three into a wall of text.

---

## A — Clarify (vague brief)

Use when they have not given a system, a stop condition, or a deliverable.

```
I can help if we pin the scope first. I build operator-ready automations (official APIs / documented connectors, plus a short SOP). I do not scrape and I do not automate likes, follows, or views.

Could you answer:
1. {{QUESTION_1}}
2. {{QUESTION_2}}

Once those are clear I will reply on this same thread with a milestone outline. Please keep the conversation here.
```

---

## B — Decline (not a fit)

Use for engagement farms, scraping-without-API, ungated social posting, or “pretend you are in the US”.

```
Thank you for sending this. I am going to pass.

I only take work I can run on official APIs or documented connectors, with a human approve step before anything public goes out. {{ONE_SPECIFIC_DETAIL}} sits outside that.

I will not submit a bid that I cannot stand behind. Wishing you a cleaner match.
```

If the UI has a **Not interested** / **Decline** control (Fiverr Briefs, some invites), use that control. Do not also spam the client.

---

## C — Stay on-platform (they asked for WhatsApp / email / off-site pay)

Rewrite the desk name. Do not then paste your number “just this once”.

### Upwork

```
I want to keep this on Upwork Messages until a contract is in place. Sharing contact details or moving payment off Upwork before that is against Upwork’s rules, and I will not do it.

If you want to continue, we can scope {{ONE_SPECIFIC_DETAIL}} here. After a contract starts, we can use whatever the contract allows.
```

### Fiverr

```
Please keep this on Fiverr. I cannot share email, phone, or WhatsApp to take the job off-platform, and I cannot take payment outside Fiverr.

If you still want the work, reply here with {{QUESTION_1}} and I will send a custom offer in this inbox.
```

### Contra

```
Let’s keep this on Contra. I can send a project proposal with scope and on-platform payment once {{QUESTION_1}} is clear.

I will not move a Contra-originated project to off-platform chat or payment from this thread.
```

---

## Do not add

- Their personal email “so we can talk faster”
- A Calendly / Telegram handle
- A threat (“I will report you”) as the first line — report in the official report tool if you need to, separately
