# Fiverr AI guidelines — this Gig's compliance map

Pack date: 2026-09-16
Gig: n8n automation + operator docs (human-delivered)
Mode: unpublished draft paste

This is not an "AI content mill" Gig. The product is a **custom n8n workflow plus
docs**, designed, tested, and documented by the freelancer. AI inside the workflow
is **opt-in**.

## No fake xAI (and no fake lab badges)

Do not write, imply, or paste any of the following on Fiverr:

- Employee, contractor, partner, or "official expert" of **xAI**
- "Grok-powered", "xAI certified", "built by xAI", or xAI / Grok logos
- Partner / employee / certified badges for n8n, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or Fiverr
  unless a later human pack confirms a real, current badge
- A US/EU employer location that is not true
- Inflated years, client counts, or earnings

Allowed: independent freelancer; n8n is the **tool**; optional light model calls
(classify / summarize / draft) that the **client** requests; public work at
yutalab.dev and Autopilot Log.

The buyer-facing FAQ states the affiliation denial in one sentence. Do not
keyword-stuff "xAI" into the title or tags.

## Fiverr rules this paste follows

Sources (public Help Center, 2026-09-15 check):

1. [Using AI on Fiverr](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593-Using-AI-on-Fiverr-Guidelines-for-freelancers-and-clients)
   - AI may assist; it is not a substitute for the freelancer's skill and effort.
   - Deliveries must be high-quality, original, and customized.
   - Generic, unmodified, or reused AI output is not acceptable.
   - Honor an explicit client request for non-AI work.
2. [Community Standards: AI-generated content](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243564776593-Community-Standards-AI-generated-content)
   - Customized work per order; no bulk identical AI content.
   - No deepfakes, fake accounts, imposters, misinformation, or non-consensual content.
   - Comply with third-party tool terms and applicable law.
3. [Prohibited services](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/49174165608593-Prohibited-services-on-Fiverr)
   - No impersonation, synthetic likeness of real people without rights, or concealed deception.
4. [Creating a Gig](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451397-Creating-a-Gig) / [Gig policies](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011421218-Gig-policies)
   - English description required.
   - Gallery images must be owned; no scraped internet images.
   - No third-party logos that imply a partnership you do not have.

## If the create-Gig form shows an AI toggle

- **Do not** classify this Gig as "AI-generated images / video / voice" or similar
  content-mill types. This is Programming & Tech → Automations & Workflows.
- If there is a generic "I use AI tools to assist" disclosure, it may be **on**,
  because FAQ 1 already says AI is optional and human-reviewed.
- If the only control is "this Gig is AI-generated content" with no assist vs
  generate split, leave it **off** and keep FAQ 1. Do not misrepresent a custom
  engineering Gig as a bulk AI asset Gig.

## Out of scope forever on this Gig

- Deepfakes, voice clones, celebrity likeness
- Fake social proof, fake accounts, engagement farms (likes, follows, views)
- Scraping a platform with no official API (including Fiverr itself)
- Unattended public posts on the client's social accounts
- Selling unmodified ChatGPT / Grok / Claude dumps as the delivery
