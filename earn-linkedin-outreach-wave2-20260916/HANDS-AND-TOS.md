# HANDS + ToS — LinkedIn Wave 2 (no automation)

**HANDS** means: you read **this** profile or **this** inbound Services request, you rewrite the one specific line, you paste in the official composer, you decide later whether to click send. A computer must not be the thing that submits.

Wave 2 adds **theme** files (n8n, AI ops, lead classify) in EN and JP. It does not relax LinkedIn’s rules on invitations, spam, or bots.

## Forbidden in this pack (do not add later either)

Do **not** write or run:

- Invitation blasts, “open profile, connect, next” loops, or CSV uploaders
- Browser automation, headless browsers, scrapers, or extensions that send faster than a person
- Third-party “LinkedIn helper”, growth tools, or InMail sequencers ([prohibited software](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387), [automated activity](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567))
- A second LinkedIn account to dodge invitation limits
- Promotional connection notes to people you do not know ([Professional Community Policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies) — spam)
- InMail from this pack (Premium). Do not start a trial to unlock it
- Unsolicited DMs that dump Services copy on someone who did not request a proposal
- Email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack in the paste
- Live USD / JPY amounts committed in git
- Fake reviews, fake engagement, or “like-for-like”

Excessive invitations plus suspected automation can restrict the account ([a548476](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548476)).

## Official lines this pack is obeying

Read the live page before arguing with a composer. Help text moves; the links are the source.

### Invitations

- [Personalize invitations to connect](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153) — Basic: note **200 characters**; personalized notes **up to 3 / month**; Premium: unlimited personalized notes (connection limits may still apply). Current page does not restate a Premium character count.
- [Differences between InMail and personalized invitations](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6239760) — InMail is Premium; older text said Basic 200 / five notes, Premium 300. **Live composer wins. This pack writes to 200 and does not buy Premium.**
- User Agreement “Dos and Don’ts”: no scripts/robots to scrape profiles or to send messages ([User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)).

### InMail (out of pack)

- [InMail character limits](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a411986) — 200 subject, 1900 body. Do not use.

### Services marketplace

- [Get started as a service provider](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550345) — request/proposal model; members can contact regardless of degree.
- [Offer services on a personal profile](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a569554) — do not switch to a Company Page; that choice is not currently reversible.
- [Respond to requests](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a570605) — Admin view → New requests → Submit proposal **or** decline (decline is irreversible).
- [Best practices for writing a proposal](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a570671) — be brief; reference the client’s request; do not invent availability.
- [Offer and request services responsibly](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1652739) — User Agreement + PCP; LinkedIn does not endorse the services.
- [Denied RFPs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a798014) — requests that include contact information can be denied; talk on LinkedIn first.
- Marketplace entry: https://www.linkedin.com/services

Help also says providers and clients may finalize terms outside LinkedIn **after** they are in a real project thread. That is not permission to put a mailto in the first proposal.

### Spam / authenticity

- [Professional Community Policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies) — no untargeted or promotional invitation notes; no artificial engagement.

## Allowed (this pack)

- Draft in git with placeholders
- One human-personalized note per person, only with a real public hook
- One human-personalized Services reply per **inbound** RFP
- Declining scraping, engagement fraud, ungated send/publish, or KYC-by-proxy
- Linking **public** portfolio / GitHub in a **Services** message (not in a connection note)
- Stopping at KYC / Premium / Save-if-viewable
